About the Foundation of Goodness

Game Goodness is an initiative of the Foundation of Goodness, a non-profit that helps children grow into kind, confident and thoughtful people. The Foundation believes learning should feel safe, joyful and open to everyone, so it builds gentle, values-led experiences that put good character first, with nothing dark, scary, or for sale.

These three games are part of that work. Each one is shaped around the Foundation's Good Life Ladders idea, where small everyday choices, like showing empathy or playing fair, become the steps a child climbs while having fun. The goal is simple: that time spent on a screen can also be time spent becoming a little kinder.
